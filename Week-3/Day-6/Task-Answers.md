# Task Answers – Day 6

## Why is data modeling important?

Data modeling organizes business information and creates relationships between records.

---

## Why should duplicate records be avoided?

Duplicate records cause reporting errors, confusion, and poor data quality.

---

## How do Flows help businesses?

Flows automate repetitive tasks and improve efficiency without coding.

---

## Why customize Salesforce objects?

Customization allows Salesforce to match specific business requirements.

# Task Answers – Day 2

## Why is Apex important?

Apex allows developers to implement custom business logic that cannot be achieved using only configuration tools.

---

## Why is SOQL important?

SOQL helps retrieve data efficiently from Salesforce objects for business operations and reporting.

---

## Why are Flows useful?

Flows automate repetitive tasks and improve productivity without requiring code.

---

## Why are Triggers needed?

Triggers automatically execute business logic when data changes occur.

---

## Why is LWC important?

LWC enables developers to create modern, reusable, and high-performance user interfaces.

---

## Why is Security important?

Security protects sensitive business data and ensures proper access control within enterprise systems.

---

## How do Salesforce technologies work together?

Objects store data, SOQL retrieves data, Apex processes data, Flows automate actions, and LWC provides the user interface.
